Nothing in this file is a legal conclusion. Questions of law appear as questions and are not answered, and the words unlawful, illegal, fraudulent and non-compliant are not used as findings anywhere in it.

Where an EVIDENCED row and a NOT ESTABLISHED row sit on the same subject, the narrower row governs that subject. The pair is deliberate and the two rows are not alternatives.
